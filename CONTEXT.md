# Tefillok

### Put Hashem Before the Scroll

Your phone is designed to capture your attention.

Every notification, every reel, every endless scroll pulls you away from what matters most.

Tefillok changes that.

Before Instagram opens. Before TikTok loads. Before YouTube starts playing.

You pause.

You pray.

Then you unlock.

Tefillok is a Hebrew-first focus app built for Jews who want to strengthen their connection to Judaism while reducing mindless screen time.

---

## The Experience

### Step 1: Download the App

When a new user opens Tefillok for the first time, they don't immediately see a paywall.

Instead, they experience a cinematic onboarding journey.

Beautiful animations, meaningful Jewish imagery, and powerful messages explain:

* How much of life is lost to scrolling
* How technology constantly competes for our attention
* Why a few moments of prayer can change an entire day
* How small spiritual habits create long-term growth

The onboarding isn't about selling.

It's about helping users understand why the app exists.

---

### Step 2: Personalized Setup

After the introduction, users answer a few questions:

* Have you used prayer or mindfulness apps before?
* How connected do you currently feel to Judaism?
* What distracts you the most?
* What would you like to improve?

Options include:

* Focus
* Gratitude
* Faith
* Consistency
* Reducing screen time
* Daily prayer habits

Users then choose which types of content they want:

* Tehillim
* Daily prayers
* Torah wisdom
* Jewish reflections
* Quotes from Chazal
* Personal prayers

---

### Step 3: Choose Apps to Lock

Users select apps they want to control:

* Instagram
* TikTok
* YouTube
* Facebook
* Games
* Other distracting apps

The app shows exactly how the system works.

---

### Step 4: Subscription

After the user understands the value of the app and has completed setup, the subscription screen appears.

Tefillok requires an active subscription.

There is no free version of the product.

Users may receive:

* A 7-day free trial
* Monthly subscription
* Annual subscription

Once subscribed, the full app becomes available.

---

## Daily Usage

When the user tries to open a locked app:

1. Tefillok intercepts the launch.
2. A Hebrew reflection screen appears.
3. The user selects their current mood.

Examples:

* Stressed
* Anxious
* Grateful
* Lonely
* Happy
* Distracted
* Tired

The app then provides relevant Hebrew content.

Examples:

* A short prayer
* A Psalm from Tehillim
* A Torah teaching
* A reflection question
* A gratitude prompt

The user spends a short moment engaging with the content.

After completion:

The app unlocks.

The user continues with intention instead of impulse.
